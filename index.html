Biography

SA KHANT NYAR, also known as KO KHANT, サーキャンニャー and (キャン), is a Myanmar-born young professional based in Japan.

He is building his experience in the hospitality field while continuing to improve his Japanese language skills and understanding of Japanese-style hospitality, including customer service, guest relations and hotel operations.

Through hotel-related training and internship experience in Japan, including practical experience in Okinawa, he has developed a strong interest in the hospitality industry and in creating positive guest experiences.

Outside of work and study, he is interested in travel, fashion, fitness, photography and lifestyle content related to his experiences in Japan. Football has also been a major part of his life: he played competitively from age 13 to 20, earned domestic medals, became a regular starter for an U18 club before turning 16, and was selected for a preliminary Myanmar U18 national-team squad/list.
